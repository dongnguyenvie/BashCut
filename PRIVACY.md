# BashCut Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 5, 2026

BashCut is a free, open-source video editor for macOS maintained by Nolan (Dong Nguyen). This policy explains how the app handles your information, including when you choose to use optional agents, plugins, or online services.

## Editing on your Mac

The built-in editor processes media on your Mac. Project files, imported or referenced media, captions, voiceover recordings, previews, caches, editing history, and exported videos are stored locally in the locations used by the app or selected by you. BashCut does not automatically upload your projects or media to the developer.

You do not need a BashCut account to use the built-in editor. BashCut does not include advertising or advertising tracking, and the core editor does not send usage analytics to the developer.

## Permissions

BashCut accesses files and folders you open or grant it access to. If you use voiceover recording, it requests microphone access and saves the recording into your project. You can manage microphone and file access in macOS System Settings. Denying microphone access prevents recording but does not prevent ordinary video editing.

## Optional agents and plugins

When you choose to use an AI agent, cloud provider, or plugin, that component may process prompts, conversation history, project context, file paths, media, or other information you provide or allow it to access. An online provider may receive that information to carry out your request. Its own privacy policy, account settings, and retention practices apply.

Only enable integrations you trust and review the information you make available to them. Features and available plugins differ by distribution channel; App Store and TestFlight builds only run plugins bundled with the app.

## Network requests and external links

Some features contact GitHub to retrieve release information, agent skills, or plugin resources, depending on the distribution channel and feature used. GitHub and its hosting infrastructure receive ordinary connection information such as your IP address when handling these requests. App Store and TestFlight copies rely on Apple for app updates instead of the editor's GitHub app update check.

Opening support, source code, or other external links connects you to those services, whose privacy policies apply. Apple separately handles App Store and TestFlight distribution and any diagnostics you choose to share with Apple.

## Information you send for support

If you email the maintainer or submit a GitHub issue, the information you choose to include is used to respond to your request and investigate the problem. GitHub issues in the public repository are public. Do not post passwords, API keys, private footage, or other sensitive information in an issue.

## Retention and your choices

You control your local project and media files and can remove them using Finder. Removing the app alone may leave project files, settings, caches, and integration data on your Mac. Files in folders managed by a cloud storage service may also be synced under that service's settings.

For information sent to an optional online provider, use that provider's account controls or contact the provider about access, retention, or deletion. For information you sent directly to the maintainer for support, contact the email below to request access, correction, or deletion, subject to any applicable retention requirements.
